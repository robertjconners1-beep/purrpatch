Why Cat Grass Works:

Cats have been eating grass instinctively for thousands of years — not because something is wrong, but because grass fiber actively helps move ingested fur through the digestive tract before it can accumulate into a blockage. Unlike petroleum gel, grass is something a cat’s body already recognizes and seeks out. It works with their natural behavior instead of asking them to tolerate something foreign.

How Cat Grass Actually Works

The science behind why cats need daily grass

As grass moves through your cat’s digestive system, the fiber carries loose fur along with it — reducing the amount that bunches up and comes back the other way. Think of it as a daily internal reset that happens the moment your cat chews.

The Science of Cat Grass Behavior

A UC Davis survey of over 1,000 cat owners found that 71% of cats regularly seek out grass or plants — and more than 90% showed no signs of illness beforehand. The study is early and small, but it offers the first biological explanation for something veterinary practitioners have observed for over a century.
